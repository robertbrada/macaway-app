MacAway

Your Mac locks the moment you walk away from it.

Download for macOS

macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

How it works

It follows the phone already in your pocket

Nothing to install on an iPhone or Apple Watch signed in to the same Apple ID as your Mac. MacAway measures how far away it is over Bluetooth, once a second.

Stand up, and it locks

Roughly ten seconds from leaving your desk to a locked screen. Come back before it fires and the countdown stops.

When it cannot see you, it locks anyway

A flat battery, Bluetooth switched off, a phone left in another room: none of those are evidence you are still there, so MacAway treats them as leaving.

Every lock explains itself

The menu tells you which device caused a lock and what it read. If it was wrong, one click corrects the setting that got it wrong.

Worth knowing

MacAway never unlocks your Mac and never stores your password. It locks, and you type your password as usual. There is no account, no server and nothing is sent anywhere — the measurement happens on your Mac and stays there.

Early software

This is an early release and it has been used on a small number of Macs. It errs towards locking: you should expect the occasional lock you did not need, and the app asks you about each one so the settings can be corrected.
